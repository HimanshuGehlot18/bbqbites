About BBQ Bites

Welcome to BBQ Bites, where passion for grilling meets the finest ingredients. Our journey started with a simple love for smoky, savory, and delicious barbecue. We believe that great food brings people together, and we're dedicated to serving up memorable meals that you can share with your family and friends.

Help & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about ordering, delivery, or our dishes? Find quick answers right here.

How do I place an order?

Browse our Menu, click "Order Now" on your favorite meals to add them to your cart, then proceed to Checkout to enter your delivery details.

What payment methods do you accept?

We support instant zero-fee UPI payments (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM) by scanning our QR code or using our UPI ID, as well as Cash on Delivery.

How long does delivery take?

Our kitchen and dispatch team are primed for speed! Most orders are prepared fresh and delivered piping hot to your doorstep within 15–30 minutes.

How can I contact customer support?

You can reach our team anytime through our Contact Page or by emailing us directly at contact@bbqbites.com.
